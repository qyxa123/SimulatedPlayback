SimulatedPlayback

Pause a video, track virtual progress, and resume at a later position.

Chromium extension source Android integration sample

Choose when to skip ahead

Simulation pauses actual playback. Elapsed time and playback speed determine the position used when you resume. The skipped interval has no sound or picture.

  • Start, resume, or discard a session from the browser popup.
  • Optional hidden-tab and muted-video triggers are off by default.
  • Per-tab browser sessions end on navigation, media change, reload, or close.
  • Android provides an app-level ExoPlayer integration, not a system framework patch.

Scope and limitations

The extension targets seekable, finite-duration YouTube and Bilibili videos. Firefox support is not verified. Live playback is outside the supported scope: known live indicators are detected, but unfamiliar finite DVR streams may evade detection. Detected protected content or ads are rejected.

Pausing a media element does not guarantee that a website stops requesting media. Battery savings, zero traffic, and instant resume have not been established. Resuming may require buffering or pressing Play.
