Title: SimPlay – Simulated Playback

Short Summary:
Pause a video, track virtual elapsed progress, and resume at a later position.

Description:
An experimental Chromium extension for YouTube and Bilibili on-demand video.
Start simulation manually, resume at the calculated position, or discard it.
Optional hidden-tab and muted-video triggers are off by default.
Simulation skips content; it does not play audio or video in the background.
Sessions are per tab and end on navigation, source change, reload, or close.

Limits:
Finite-duration, seekable media is required. Known live indicators and detected
protected content or advertisements are rejected; some finite DVR streams may
evade detection. Live playback is outside the supported scope. Website streaming
requests can continue while paused; zero data use and battery savings are
not guaranteed. Resume may buffer or require pressing Play on the website.

This file is draft listing text, not evidence of a published store listing.
Use actual screenshots of the tested build for any store submission;
the repository's existing SVG images are illustrative mockups.

Permissions:
- storage: save opt-in hidden-tab and muted-video preferences locally.
- Content scripts: run on YouTube and Bilibili to control the page's video.
Virtual progress remains in the current tab's page session, without analytics
or remote storage. No notification or broad tab-access permission is requested.
